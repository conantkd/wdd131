Rema Fisioterapia

W05 Project: Website Planning Document

Site Name

Rema Fisioterapia

This name reflects the actual brand name of a local business that specializes in physical therapy and rehabilitation services. It aligns with their logo and business identity.

Optional domain: remafisio.com

Site Purpose

The website will:

  • Showcase the clinic's services, staff, and facilities.
  • Allow patients to schedule appointments online.
  • Display information about physical therapy techniques and rehabilitation programs.
  • Provide contact details and location information.

Scenarios

  1. Do you provide physical therapy at home in Bello, Medellín, or Envigado?
  2. Which physical therapy services can help with my condition?
  3. How do I book an appointment with a Rema physiotherapist?
  4. What areas of the Valle de Aburrá do you serve?
  5. What should I expect during my first at-home therapy session?
  6. How can I contact Rema to ask about treatment or availability?
